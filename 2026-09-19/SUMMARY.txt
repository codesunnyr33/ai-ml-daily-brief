# AI/ML Daily Brief — 2026-09-19 (PT)

First daily brief. Sources checked mid–late September 2026.

## 4. Markets where AI/ML is still underused (sell opportunities)

- **Construction & built world SMEs:** huge GDP share, low digitization, long procurement; demand for scheduling, safety monitoring, document/BIM copilots that plug into existing field workflows.
- **Manufacturing & maintenance (mid-market):** MaintainX-style maintenance ops, quality inspection, downtime prediction — labor shortage + thin margins favor ROI-clear agents over generic chatbots.
- **Agriculture / field ops:** weather, pest, soil, market intel via simple WhatsApp/voice/SMS interfaces where smartphone-only distribution dominates.
- **Insurance claims & underwriting ops:** high-volume semi-structured paperwork still human-heavy; vertical agents for FNOL triage, coverage check, underwriting packets.
- **Healthcare admin (prior auth, clinical documentation):** Abridge-class documentation is early; prior-authorization evidence assembly and SMB clinic back-office remain under-automated.
- **Legal / audit / personal injury ops:** contract triage and matter workflows (Legora, EvenUp, Fieldguide pattern) — still room in mid-market firms without enterprise AI budgets.
- **Property / facilities management & local services:** EliseAI-style housing/healthcare ops; residential services, transaction coordination, supplier risk — fragmented buyers, high labor line to displace.
